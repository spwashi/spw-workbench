📚 Theory

          

            The semantic features model is grounded in embodied cognition research:
          

          

            Intensity (FORCE)

            Active ↔ Passive (muscle tension, energy)
          

          

            Proximity (NEAR-FAR)

            Engaged ↔ Distant (proprioception, reach)
          

          

            Clarity (FOCUS)

            Sharp ↔ Diffuse (visual acuity, attention)

            Active transformation: high intensity, high proximity, medium clarity
