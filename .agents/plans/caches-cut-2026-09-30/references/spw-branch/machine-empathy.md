# Machine empathy and tunable complexity in the Spw workbench\n\nLabels: **(a)** canon says it · **(b)** code does it (verified this pass unless marked 'per study') · **(c)** proposal.\n\n## 1. What machine empathy means here\n\nMachine empathy is not sentiment detection and not a model of the person. Canon forbids that: inspection evidence 'never grants an effect, infers a person-level trait, or becomes an authority-facing score' (a, `.spw/conventions/cli.spw:121`), and the cut adds that 'no tool scores the reader' (a, `contract.spw` ^[marginalia].standing).\n\nOperationally (c): the tool holds a **declared reader context** and lets it choose what is disclosed, what is warned about, and what is recorded, while product identity stays fixed. This extends a stack canon already has. Representational disclosure says context 'is not ambient vibes — it is a declared stack' of dialect × mode × grain × channel (a, `docs/theory/spw/representational-disclosure.spw:96-122`), and 'Disclosure never renames identity' (a, `:45`). Machine empathy adds three declared coordinates:\n\n1. **Care**: how much attention this pass will spend, on the canon ladder glance, skim, read, audit, adversarial (a, `vocabulary.spw:47`, gloss `:65`).\n2. **Habit**: which prior notation is pulling the reader's eye (C, TeX, CSS, R/Stata, MARC, timecode, chat). The persona study measured that pull glyph by glyph: 32 forms × 12 clusters, most cells false_friend (c, built on (b) probes).\n3. **Practice**: whether the reader wants answers shown, or wants to predict first and then see the parser's answer (c; tokens §2, render §5.4).\n\nA tool can know three things without guessing at a person:\n\n- **What it omitted.** The seed already ships three shapes for this:\n  - `CacheLayerCard{present, omission, next}` (b, `packages/spw-seed/src/ir/cache-layer.ts:26-34`)\n  - `ProgressiveProductRecord.completeness{included, omitted}` plus `deferred` (b, `ir/progressive.ts:12-38`)\n  - `ONFValueProjectionReceipt.omissions` (b, `normalize-construction.ts:48-79`). The interpreter attaches this receipt whenever the value reading drops postfix attachments (b, `spw-runtime/src/interpreter/interpreter.ts:380-386`).\n\n  Canon's omission law requires every bounded product to state its losses (a, `cli.spw:100`).\n- **Where a habit will mislead.** Each familiarity cell rests on a parse probe. `vm: -70` binds 70 and hides a lexer error; `x: a ? b : c` parses completely and means something else. Re-run this pass (b): `spw inspect source --through tokens` reports 2 errors for `-70` and `café`, and 0 for the ternary.\n- **What care was already applied.** Receipts name the care actually applied (a, `contract.spw` ^[inspection]). The live cut holds 768 receipts in 243 of 245 files: glance 119, skim 258, read 107, audit 248, adversarial 36 (b, counted 2026-10-02).\n\n**Where today's tools fail empathy (b).**\n\n- Lexer errors leave `success` true. The LSP then publishes nothing (`packages/spw-lsp/src/handlers/analysis.ts:51-52`), and the validator passes the file (`scripts/analyzers/spw-syntax-validate.ts:209,227`).\n- `spw outline` skips every `#` line and takes depth from `floor(indent/2)` (`packages/spw-cli/src/view.ts:231,234`). Its aliases are `skim` and `read` (`commands.ts:339`), so the command named read delivers less than the care rung named read.\n- `emit pack --host brief` drops claim status. Verified on a skeleton door: the brief kept title, audience, claim, proof and door, and the `#!settled` mood disappeared.\n\n**Owner holds** (a, `.spw/caches/resume/2026-09-30-direction.spw:30-31,66-77`):\n\n- A newcomer lands on one fragment door. There is no command tour.\n- Charge stays off the visit arc.\n- Language, mathematics, physics, linguistics and attention lead.\n\nEmpathy therefore never escalates disclosure on its own. Canon already defers 'weight-driven auto grain without explicit user/agent choice' (a, `representational-disclosure.spw:296`). Valence never marks progress (c).\n\n## 2. Tunable complexity: three ladders, kept apart\n\n- **Care** (a) is how much attention an inspection spends.\n- **Level** (a, the vocabulary `level` set) is who a surface is written for. The plan's Propaedia tiers attach to it:\n  - The class index is the outline, with a ^[ladder] running intro → foundation → advanced → graduate → practitioner.\n  - The glossary is the Micropaedia.\n  - The leaves are the Macropaedia.\n- **Grain** (b, `ir/granularity.ts:14`: `skim | card | field | full`) is the disclosure aperture canon sanctions (a, `representational-disclosure.spw:111-116`). Ladder rungs are 'not developmental stage' (a, `form-ladders.spw:81`).\n\nThese three are orthogonal (c). An intro reader may audit a graduate surface, and a practitioner may glance at an intro door. Empathy uses each one differently (c):\n\n- level picks the default vocabulary (spoken names, glossary links)\n- care picks which IR is shown and what the receipt records\n- grain is how the tool materializes that choice\n\n**Homonym hazard (b).** 'skim' names three different things:\n\n1. the care rung that reads frames\n2. the granularity depth below `card`, where the resolver forces the bytecode plane (`granularity.ts:198-201`)\n3. a CLI alias of `outline` (`commands.ts:339`). The outline header even prints `# spw skim` (verified).\n\nRule (c): map between vocabularies and never rename one into another. Every surface names which vocabulary it is using.\n\n**Disclosure ladder (c; every 'today' cell is b).**\n\n| care | reader sees | IR | producer today | hides today |\n|---|---|---|---|---|\n| glance | emit card: title, claim, door, cold_open | emit pack (9 hosts, `spw-cli/src/emit/types.ts:21-31`) | `spw emit pack --host brief` | status, limits, provenance |\n| skim | frames and landmarks | line scan, no parse | `spw outline` | anchors, particles, wonders, cold_open |\n| read | every claim with its particles bound, limits, wonders | AST + particle bindings (`canonical/particles.ts:82,148`) | LSP hover and tokens; `spw expand` | errors when success is true; LSP paint is regex, not parse (per tokens study) |\n| audit | sources, registry cells, links, numbers | AST + resolve graph + registry cells | cut gate + `spw resolve` (the trusted pair) | census and graph count `#fragment` refs as broken (per render study, `corpus.ts:196-209`) |\n| adversarial | bytes, tokens, gaps, value projections, counter-sources | tokens, gaps, fingerprints, ONF value receipts | `spw inspect source --through tokens`, `spw fingerprint --expr`, specimens | nothing; this rung lists losses |\n\n**Care ascends while IR descends (c, grounded in b).** Glance reads the most derived product; adversarial care reads the least derived one. That is because the silent failures live below the AST:\n\n- Lexer errors keep `success` true.\n- ONF keeps source spans only on postfix constructions (`types/ast/onf.ts:20-26`). Counted this pass (b): 0 of 376 nodes on `water.spw`, 0 of 522 on `contract.spw`, and 2 of 16 on a postfix sample.\n\nTunable complexity is therefore not 'more detail means a deeper tree'. At the top rung the tool hands back the raw layer, with its losses itemized.\n\n**Habit and practice dials (c).**\n\n- Prior-notation profiles warn on familiar-but-wrong readings without changing the parse, the way `-W` flags do (persona q33). Specimens are their fixtures.\n- Practice modes are predicates over one parse-derived role stream (tokens §2). Predict-then-reveal logs accuracy only to a reader-owned file (a, `cli.spw:118-121`; contract arcs.people).\n- Fading follows the expertise reversal effect (Kalyuga, Ayres, Chandler & Sweller 2003, *Educational Psychologist* 38(1):23-31).\n- The highlighting evidence is mixed: Sarkar 2015 (n=10) against Hannebauer, Hesenius & Gruhn 2018 (n=390). The honest claim is 'may help past the novice stage; unmeasured for Spw' (c).\n\n## 3. IRs as graded inspection surfaces\n\n**Inventory (b).**\n\n- source bytes\n- tokens: 32 types; gap classes tight, open, cadence and episode\n- AST: 28 node types\n- particle bindings and the deixis table, which the gate reads (`spw-cut-gate.ts:245`)\n- ONF σ(args)[frames] (`onf.ts:78-85`)\n- the value-projection receipt\n- the IR kind spine: 28 kinds and 9 edge kinds (`ir/kinds.ts:9-56`)\n- `IrRef` identity, which excludes the retention stamp `bornBeat` (`ir/ref.ts:61-83`)\n- cache-layer cards for four planes\n- progressive products\n- emit packs\n- the corpus product (a, `cache-field.spw:72-82`)\n\nCanon keeps identity, retention, disclosure and attention on separate planes (a, `cache-field.spw:3`). An inspection surface must therefore say which plane it reads.\n\n**What an inspection receipt records.**\n\n- **Today** (a): event, at, agent, care, outcome. The gate checks only that these values come from the vocabulary (b, `spw-cut-gate.ts:308-313`). It does not check the honesty rule that `#:review` summarizes the highest passing care (a, `contract.spw` ^[inspection].honesty).\n- **A heuristic check** (c) mapping spot_checked→skim, sourced→audit and adversarial_checked→adversarial flags 4 of 245 surfaces: two with no receipts, and two sourced surfaces whose best passing care is skim or read.\n- **Proposed fields** (c):\n  - `ir`: which IR was read\n  - `through`: the canonical command used\n  - `scope`: the anchors or line ranges covered\n  - `omitted`: what was not inspected\n  - `digest`: a sha256 of the bytes inspected, tying receipts to the manifest's fixity\n  - `next`: where care ran out, so the next reader starts there (a, `contract.spw` ^[inspection].unpredictability)\n\n  Each field borrows a shipped shape: omission and next from `CacheLayerCard`, included and omitted from progressive products, and omissions from the ONF receipt.\n\nA receipt is a **loss ledger, not a grade**. The reader writes it and keeps it local (a, `cli.spw:118-121`).\n\n**Roles on the ladder (b today → c).**\n\n- **Gate** (audit). Today it fails on parse errors, prose fallback, capsule swallows and unresolved refs. Add: review honesty, receipt fields, and axis vocabularies.\n- **Emit** (glance, and the publishing gate). Today it drops status, and audio never speaks the claim (per render study, `emit/codecs.ts:282-290`). Add a status glyph and a spoken claim.\n- **Expand** (read and compare). Today it transcludes digests, but its provenance is a `#` comment, the transcluded `#>` is lost, and links are left unrebased (per tokens study, `expand.ts:79,94-102`). Add segments with origins, so every projected byte maps to source (law L2, verified by the tokens study).\n- **Census and graph** (audit, corpus scale). Untrustworthy on fragment refs until fixed. Add a reader-owned predict card (`census --blank`, `--against`).\n- **LSP** (read, in the editor). Today it parses without a path and keeps trace events it never reads (per tokens study, `server-index.ts:371`). Add a parse-derived role stream, a care setting, and diagnostics for lexer errors.\n\n## 4. Minimal implementation path (c; ranked; tooling lands outside wave 3)\n\n1. **Honesty floor.** Publish `pr.errors` whatever `success` says (`analysis.ts:51-52`), and fail the validator when `errorCount > 0` (`spw-syntax-validate.ts:209,227`). Land this after path-aware parsing (`server-index.ts:371`); otherwise 71 wip files flood with errors. Effort S.\n2. **Receipt honesty.** A gate check that `#:review` does not exceed the highest passing care, plus optional receipt fields ir, scope, omitted, digest and next (`spw-cut-gate.ts:308-316`). Effort S.\n3. **Outline discloses itself.** Show anchors, claim moods, wonders and cold_open; take depth from braces; print a line counting what was omitted (`view.ts:222-309`). Effort S/M.\n4. **Declared care.** `resolveGranularity({care})` maps care to depth, plane and follow (`granularity.ts:49-61,172-229`). Expose it as `--care` on outline, emit and inspect, and as an LSP setting. It never escalates on its own. Effort S/M.\n5. **Glance keeps status.** A status glyph in the brief, and audio that speaks the claim (`emit/codecs.ts:276-300`). Effort S/M.\n6. **One role stream.** `canonical/roles.ts` (`tokenRoles` → `RoleSpan[]`) feeds semantic tokens and the practice modes. Effort M.\n7. **Habit profiles.** `spw lint --habits c,tex,css,…`, with specimens as the fixtures. Effort M.\n8. **Projection segments.** `canonical/projection.ts`, so expand and reduce emit loss receipts. Effort M.\n\nTests ride with each step:\n\n- `café: 1` gives a diagnostic.\n- A sourced surface with only skim receipts fails the gate.\n- A door's outline lists its wonder.\n- `--care adversarial` on a door prints its tokens and gaps.
