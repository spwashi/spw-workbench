# Plan: ecosystem-surface-governance

Turn reusable surface archetypes into an operable governance contract without binding them to domains, ownership, or a predicted deployment form.

## Goal

Preserve the transferable functions once explored through named public surfaces—orientation, curriculum, inquiry, publishing, observation, exchange, and reflection—while describing readiness through stewardship, evidence, and interaction contracts. Governance should coordinate install, DX, CLI, editor, release, contributor-formation, and research plans without making speculative identities or destinations into release requirements.

Taste note: portable function, honest stewardship, and readiness evidence.

## Scope

- **In scope:** Maintain `.spw/surfaces/surface-archetypes.spw` and its index; define readiness and installability; route adjacent plans through evidence-backed governance; keep the registry discoverable.
- **Out of scope:** Naming or registering domains, predicting publication shape, assigning ownership, implementing runtime/editor features, or treating an archetype as a launch commitment.

## Craft guard

- Describe a surface by the author task, relationship, and evidence it enables.
- Do not encode a specific identifier, owner, host, or presumed publication form.
- Require observable readiness evidence; feature counts and editor parity are insufficient.
- Keep governance changes tied to real decisions rather than decorative taxonomy.

## Commits

1. `.[plans] =governance[archetypes] — align the portable surface contract`
2. `.[surfaces] =abstract[functions] — preserve ethos without identity claims`
3. `.[plans] =route[readiness] — align install, release, DX, CLI, and editors`
4. `![plans] *verify[governance] — check syntax, references, and evidence routes`
